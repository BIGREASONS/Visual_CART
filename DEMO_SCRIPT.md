## Detailed Scene-by-Scene Script

### Scene 1: The Problem — Cart Fragmentation Across Apps
- **Time**: `0:00 - 0:25` (Duration: 25 seconds)
- **Visuals**:
  - Close-up of a mobile user browsing an Instagram tech review reel showing a high-performance SSD.
  - Quick split-screen cut: User switches to YouTube, then opens Chrome to search prices, then opens Flipkart, then Amazon.
  - Fast-paced graphical overlay showing 4 separate unfinished checkout screens with a bold metric: **"70.19% of Mobile Carts are Abandoned"**.
- **On-Screen Text**:
  - *"Discovery happens everywhere. Carts are fragmented anywhere."*
  - *"70.19% Mobile Cart Abandonment Rate (Baymard Institute)"*
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Every day, we discover products in the apps we love: an Instagram reel, a YouTube tech teardown, a Reddit thread, or a web article. But the moment you want to buy, shopping breaks down. Your items end up scattered across four different retailer apps, forcing you to create new accounts, re-enter payment details, and guess who has the lowest price. Over seventy percent of mobile carts are simply abandoned. Today, we change that."*
- **Audio Cue**: Mellow ambient synth track swells gently into an energetic, rhythmic fintech beat.

### Scene 2: Visual Capture — Zero-Friction Android Ingestion
- **Time**: `0:25 - 0:50` (Duration: 25 seconds)
- **Visuals**:
  - User is browsing an image in Chrome showing the **Crucial P3 Plus 1TB PCIe 4.0 SSD**.
  - User long-presses the image or invokes **Google Circle to Search**.
  - User taps the native Android **Share** button.
  - The Android Sharesheet opens; in the Direct Share row, the Forest Green **"Add to Visual Cart"** dynamic shortcut appears prominently.
  - User taps the shortcut. The bottom popup dialog smoothly animates upward with a loading pulse: *"Analyzing visual features…"*
  - Inset shot: Quick cut showing the user toggling the **Quick Settings Tile** ("Visual Cart") which immediately deploys the floating circular bubble on screen.
- **On-Screen Text**:
  - *"Native Android Direct Share & Quick Settings Integration"*
  - *"Zero Invasive Permissions · Works Across Any App"*
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Meet PayPal Visual Cart. With native Android Direct Share and Google Circle to Search integration, saving a product takes just one tap. Whether you're in Chrome, Instagram, or YouTube, simply share the image or link directly to Visual Cart. Or, toggle our Quick Settings tile to launch a floating, draggable bubble that stays with you anywhere you browse."*
- **Audio Cue**: Subtle haptic tap chime upon selecting the shortcut.

- **Time**: `0:50 - 1:20` (Duration: 30 seconds)
- **Visuals**:
  - The Visual Cart bottom sheet transitions smoothly from loading to the **Matched Product Card**.
  - Clean Warm Ivory (`#F6F3EC`) card with Deep Ink typography:
    - Title: **Crucial P3 Plus 1TB PCIe 4.0 3D NAND NVMe M.2 SSD**
    - Brand badge: **Crucial** | Category: **Electronics**
    - Extracted Specification Tags: `[1TB]` `[PCIe 4.0]` `[NVMe 1.4]` `[M.2 2280]` `[Up to 5,000 MB/s]`
  - Green pill badge in the upper right corner glows: **"EXACT MATCH · 96% CONFIDENCE"**.
  - A small expandable card reveals the AI explanation: *"Brand, storage capacity (1TB), and PCIe 4.0 interface match exact hardware specifications."*
  - Quick animated graphic illustrating the Google Gemini 2.5 Flash structured JSON schema pipeline with local regex fallback.
- **On-Screen Text**:
  - *"Multimodal AI Vision Pipeline (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash)"*
  - *"Exact Spec Parsing: 1TB · PCIe 4.0 · NVMe M.2 2280"*
  - *"Zero Downtime: Dual-Tier On-Device Fallback Engine"*
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Under the hood, Google Gemini 2.5 Flash analyzes the image and text context, extracting a strict canonical product specification. It doesn't just recognize a generic hard drive; it disambiguates the exact model: Crucial P3 Plus, one terabyte, PCIe 4.0, NVMe M.2 2280, with a ninety-six percent confidence score. And if you're offline or have low bandwidth, our on-device heuristic engine takes over with zero downtime."*
